# 3.0.0-alpha.3

## Added

- Aggressor Mode, on by default and switchable in Game Options. A style meter in the upper left grades a run of kills from D to S, with the combo count, a bar that drains in three seconds at D and a second and a half at S, each character's best run shown and kept in their save, and a "NEW RECORD!" flourish when it is beaten. Grades step up at 25, 50, 75 and 100 kills.
- Only kills and destroyed generators extend a run. Blows landed under Time Stop, invulnerability or invisibility earn nothing. In Aggressor Mode, only melee and projectiles damage generators; super shots, turbo attacks, the thunder hammer, potions, breaths and shields still earn kills toward the run.
- The swarm answers the run. Generators near the party breed without regard to the level's quota for as long as a run lasts, faster with each grade, into a pool widened to sixty-four enemies. Enemies move, attack and breed faster and take more hits as the run grows, up to five times their usual pace, and past S rank they turn dark red and burn with a sinister aura. Dropping the run calms them at once.
- Elemental enemies. During a run, a frenzied generator may bear a fire, lightning, holy or acid enemy, with a 2% chance at D rising 2% a grade to 10% at S. It stands half again as large with three and a half times the health, shrugs off its own element and deals it with every blow, and leaves a bag holding that element's potion when slain.
- Each elemental enemy wears its element: fire licks off the body in the fire potion's own flames over breath puffs and embers; lightning crawls over it in jagged arcs while static crackles on its skin; acid wraps it in a green haze that rolls out over the ground in rings and drips off it; holy light breaks out of it in rays and glints.
- Online games carry Aggressor Mode as a host rule, so every player sees the same swarm.
- A frame spike tracer for the developer console: `spikes <milliseconds>`, `spikes auto` or `spikes off` logs any frame whose work overruns the threshold along with what the level held at that moment. `GDL_SPIKE_MS` sets it from the environment.
- With Aggressor Mode off, the original run of close blows and its two-second window remain exactly as before, without the meter.

## Fixed

- Mouse clicks in menus and shops act only on the pointed option or player panel, even when a control profile also binds that button to Select, Sell, or Exit.
- Archer turbo volleys retain equipped amulet effects and Super Shot visuals without multiplying their projectile spread.
- Potions and potion shields wake inactive enemy statues, allowing a single potion to destroy a dormant Death.
- Auto-Use Item activates Turbo pickups when enabled, without activating purchases or restored inventory.
- Potion shields retain their full height and use the original horizontal scale and lighting radius.
- Restored Sumner's secret-level countdown and time-running-out warning.
- Stopped the Frozen Camp boiler switch sound from continuing after its mechanism finishes.
- Enemy healthbars use their authored offsets, correcting the high polar bear bars.
- Elemental enemies' projectiles use their assigned element instead of combining it with the original projectile's element.
- Holding light attack with Auto Melee enabled builds the close-combat chain for stronger finishers; manual controls retain their original button-press timing.
- Pojo uses its normal attack when the heavy-attack button is pressed, matching the original controls.
- Continuous full-turn scenery animations, including Mothership fans, interpolate across loop boundaries instead of briefly snapping at higher frame rates.
