# 1.3.3-alpha.3

## Added

- The Mountain generators' lava pools take the splattered silhouette their artists drew: the generator model carries an indexed mask whose palette shipped blank, so the console drew a plain square pad. The mask's indices now cut the pad to that shape (`display.authoredSilhouettes`, on by default; off shows the square as the console did).

## Fixed

- A shot fired against a fence that only holds the party back, such as the Mountain Cavern's lava ledge, flies on instead of striking the air: the muzzle check now uses the same wall query as the weapon in flight, which passes those fences as the original's does.
- The four amulets are named by their element in the inventory and the powerup selector (Fire Amulet, Ltn. Amulet, Light Amulet, Acid Amulet) instead of all reading "Weapon": an amulet keeps its element as a number in the weapon's low nibble rather than as flag bits, so every amulet fell through to the general weapon name.
