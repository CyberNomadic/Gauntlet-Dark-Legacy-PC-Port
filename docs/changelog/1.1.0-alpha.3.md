# 1.1.0-alpha.3

## Added

- Added an optional expanded shop with Gold Invulnerability, Pojo, 5-Way Shot, Shrink, and Timestop. Each costs 1,000 gold; the new stock sits between Anti-Death and Hand of Death. Disabled by default.
- Added optional auto-saving after level and Tower shops. Characters reuse their save slots; unsaved characters receive an available slot. An animated green rune confirms a successful save. Disabled by default.
- Auto-save also records successful secret-level rewards during the exit transition, including each online player's locally owned characters.
- Added a stronger controller rumble when Thunder Hammer strikes the ground, respecting the player's rumble setting.
- Reserved Escape for pausing regardless of the selected input device; it cannot be assigned to an attack or menu-selection action.
- Synchronized online level results, shopping, and return to the Tower for ordinary campaign exits, including mouse selection and shared potion results.
- Added online secret-level visits and returns, preserving the suspended level and granting earned secret characters on each player's machine.
- Added synchronized Skorne II and Garm ending movies and results, including Garm's final statistics and the correct Tower return area.
- Added guest controller rumble for damage, melee hits, destroyed generators, and Thunder Hammer impacts, respecting local controller settings.
- Added a Ghost Town ramp scenario for checking enemies approaching the wooden deck from the side.

## Fixed

- Settings now live in `config/settings.json` beside the executable in all builds. Existing per-user settings are imported when no local settings exist, without deleting the original file.
- Newly purchased shop powerups stay inactive when returning to the Tower, regardless of the Auto-Use Item setting.
- Pausing silences switch-driven movement and turntable sounds. Resuming restores active motion cues without replaying already-finished sounds.
- Enemy death skins use their authored transparency instead of turning into opaque black shapes, while hit flashes and frozen skins retain their normal coverage.
- Stop Time consistently freezes looping scenery during entry and switch cutscenes. One-shot switch motions continue as in retail.
- Renamed Gold Invulnerability's inventory label to "G. Invuln." and corrected the capitalization of "Difficulty."
- Routed stage-entry announcement and map sounds through the Movie volume setting.
- Anti-Death holds Death stationary during a valid drain and releases him when contact, the item, or the draining player's eligibility is lost. Multiple holders are handled independently.
- Corrected enemy render interpolation sinking into sloped floors at step transitions, including Ghost Town's wooden ramps.
- Corrected enemies choosing the ground underneath raised ramps when approaching from the side, including the wooden deck near Ghost Town's gallows.
- Preserved the parent level's gold, kills, and experience statistics across secret-level visits and restored the brief loading transition on entry and return.
- Newly earned secret-character unlocks now persist globally when playing a scenario, without granting the scenario's preloaded unlocks.
- Gold pickups remain in the world when the player's wallet is full. Secret challenge coins still count toward their objective at the cap, without adding gold. Open gold chests are passable, as open food chests already were.
- Online shop transitions wait for every player and preserve each machine's save-slot ownership.
- Online shopping rejects duplicated, stale, or unauthorized commands, including delayed confirmations aimed at a different item.
- Corrected guest enemy interpolation through ramp lips and moving floors without changing the host's movement or collision.
- Discarded stale controller feedback across local pause, travel, and disconnects.
- Consolidated project attribution into LICENSE and removed the separate COPYRIGHT file; release packages continue to include both attribution and GPLv3 terms.
