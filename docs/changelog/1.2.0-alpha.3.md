# 1.2.0-alpha.3

## Added

- Local input replay for supported movement, ranged attacks, melee, turbo attacks, shields, and potion actions, with authoritative correction.
- Replay checkpoints for player and enemy animation, enemy pools, moving geometry, effects, particles, and texture clocks.
- Coordinated enemy, generator, and enemy-projectile correction with atomic restore and bounded replay.
- Portable random-number state and resource-order-independent enemy corrections.
- Bounded network checkpoints for complete enemy, generator, and enemy-projectile state.
- Predicted weapon and potion flight with release identities that prevent duplicate projectiles after confirmation.
- Input-delay telemetry and a separate-process network harness, including optional Docker network-impairment testing.
- One ordered asset scope resolves every placed item's art the way the original searches its archives: the level's own items, then the realm's, then the lent enemy and weapon archives, with the original's object-name suffix order.
- The authored draw flags are defined once and drive every object's sorting, blending and depth policy for world scenery, placed items and enemies alike.
- Generator births play the realm's birth bursts (GENFX1 to GENFX3) where the original does, such as the Dream realm's imps.
- Traps sound their own strike by realm and kind, as the original does: the Castle's tentacles, spikes, saw blades and force fields, the Forest's log spikes and swinging blades, and each realm's fire holes.
- The Mountain's Peak keeps its enemies moving during the party's entrance, as the level record asks.
- A census test builds every playable figure, every swarm kind, every great one's costume and every level's fixtures from the shipped data, and checks each level names only item kinds the loader stands.

## Fixed

- Restored guest gameplay audio and synchronized stage sound playback.
- Corrected guest enemy death presentation and animated effect playback.
- Preserved exact animation blends when correcting and replaying player actions.
- Corrected progression-ceremony camera framing for guests.
- Improved connection failure handling and diagnostics for online rooms.
- The Forest realm's first-tier generators are visible again.
- The Castle Courtyard's switches and the rest of the realm-shared switch and rotator art are visible again when a level ships its own item archive.
- Scenery such as the Castle Treasury's weapon racks no longer sorts behind the floor: objects are drawn by their authored sort flags rather than by whether a texture carries alpha.
- Pickups and animated items sort by their authored flags too, with alpha-textured pickups still composited after the solid fixtures.
- Sumner is visible and plays as a wizard once unlocked: his costume, actions and effects come from the wizard's archive.
- Enemy casters wait between casts as the level's missile rate and the difficulty setting say; the rate had been applied upside down and without the difficulty.
- Bosses no longer use attacks whose health band is closed on itself (the Dragon's split fireball, the Chimera's strafes, the Spider Queen's right whip and the Skorne fireballs), which the original reaches only through their patterns, and they sleep for two seconds after placement until the whole party is within their waking distance.
- The Forest's six-move saw blades cycle without resting, as the original cycles traps of more than four moves.
