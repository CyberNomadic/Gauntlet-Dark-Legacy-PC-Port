# 1.3.4-alpha.3

## Added

- The pre-push hook now waits seconds to minutes instead of an hour: it lints the changed units and one unit per changed header, without the clang static analyzer, and leaves the full fan-out to CI. CI's lint runs in eight shards from a cache of clean results that is kept whatever the outcome, so a push re-lints only what it reached, and a change to documents alone (README, docs/, licences) builds, tests and lints nothing.
- The Auto Aim control option is back, under Game Options > Combat and as `combat.autoAim` (on by default): off, thrown weapons, the familiar's spit and a standing attack keep the way the player faces instead of turning toward the nearest enemy, boss, barrel or wall, as the original's per-pad Auto Aim option did. An online room agrees the setting as part of its rules.
