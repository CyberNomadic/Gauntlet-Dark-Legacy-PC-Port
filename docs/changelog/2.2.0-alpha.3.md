# 2.2.0-alpha.3

## Added

- Host-controlled developer-console access in online games through `console_permissions 1` and `console_permissions 0`.
- Synchronized online console cheats, spawning, noclip, and level travel. Camera and HUD commands remain local to each player.

## Fixed

- Strong throws and melee attacks now apply the original Gargoyle knockback, including accumulated hits and the correct push direction. Area attacks retain consistent push strength at different distances.
- Floor generators, including those in Desecrated Temple, now place emerging enemies correctly.
- Restored near-camera scenery transparency. Desecrated Temple's broken column caps also fade to keep the view clear without changing their connecting shafts.
- Ancient Tree archers and bombers retain their moving-platform attachments and spawn at the correct height.
- Crystals beside walls and ledges in Mausoleum and Cliffs can be collected correctly.
- Cursor-aimed weapons and familiar projectiles adjust for target height without steering away from the cursor's horizontal direction.
- Corrected Sumner and the SUM224 cheat's starting level, supplies, armor, throw voice, and hand-glow blending. Existing saves migrate without replenishing supplies already spent by an established Sumner character.
- Prevented riders from slipping through the Shipyard elevator's corner onto passing geometry.
- Online input buffering adapts to half the measured round-trip time, up to four ticks.
- Fixed online matches occasionally stopping when a guest disconnects.
- Kept online input packets within a single network fragment, including packets carrying console commands.
