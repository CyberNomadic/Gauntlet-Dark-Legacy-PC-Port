# 1.3.2-alpha.3

## Fixed

- An exploding barrel no longer floors or pushes a player mid-turbo, special or combo: the original skips the whole of a blast's damage while the player cannot be damaged, the knockdown with it.
- A barrel holding more than one key lets out the key ring, as a chest does and as the original's shared container opening has it, instead of a single key.
- The Mountain Cavern's vermin generator no longer stands on an opaque plate: its skull pile's alpha-textured body is blended by its texture's alpha after the solids, as the original sorts every item, instead of being cut by coverage.
- Player movement resolves against walls and floors the way the original's PlayerMotion does. Walls meet a sphere as wide as the class, a unit above the collision centre, and only from their front, so a body can never be held inside one (the Sky Shipyard's railings); a wall met turns the step along it, and a second wall against the first stops it. The floor is the one under the body's centre, looked for from the collision centre down three and a half body height; a step may rise or drop three (four on the same floor), a drop within a width ahead holds the body a width short of the edge, and a deeper floor refuses the step, so ledges the original never let a walker leave are no longer walked off. A lift that holds its riders keeps them at its height until it stops.
