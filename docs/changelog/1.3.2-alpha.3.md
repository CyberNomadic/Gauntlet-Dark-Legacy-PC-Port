# 1.3.2-alpha.3

## Added

- A level debug overlay, shown and hidden with the ` key (the `debug` keyboard binding): the level's name, each player's position, heading and floor object, the follow camera's position, angles and marker, the nearest placed objects and the live enemy count. Turning it on also writes the same lines to the log.

## Fixed

- An exploding barrel no longer floors or pushes a player mid-turbo, special or combo: the original skips the whole of a blast's damage while the player cannot be damaged, the knockdown with it.
- A barrel holding more than one key lets out the key ring, as a chest does and as the original's shared container opening has it, instead of a single key.
- The Mountain Cavern's critter generator no longer stands on an opaque plate: its skull pile's alpha-textured body is blended by its texture's alpha after the solids, as the original sorts every item, instead of being cut by coverage.
- Player movement resolves against walls and floors the way the original's PlayerMotion does. Walls meet a sphere as wide as the class, a unit above the collision centre, and only from their front, so a body can never be held inside one (the Sky Shipyard's railings); a wall met turns the step along it, and a second wall against the first stops it. The floor is the one under the body's centre, looked for from the collision centre down three and a half body height; a step may rise or drop three (four on the same floor), a drop within a width ahead holds the body a width short of the edge, and a deeper floor refuses the step, so ledges the original never let a walker leave are no longer walked off. A lift that holds its riders keeps them at its height until it stops. A body a push leaves in a wall is put back out the way the original corrects a step, along the face's normal and only for faces it stands in front of, so the Sky Shipyard's one-sided crow's nest railing no longer wedges a body that brushes its angled boards.
- Enemies animated as a flip-book of meshes blend between frames at render rates above the simulation rate whatever the frames' export: vertices written in another order, split or joined between frames, are matched by their texture coordinate and colour to the nearest in the next frame, and frames with their own textures or texture coordinates blend their geometry and coordinates too. The grunts' archers, the demons, the knights, the dogs, the plants and the vermin stepped between frames before; the few pairs the original authored as cuts still cut.
- A boss arena's camera stays with the arena from the boss's fall to the level's end, as the original's does: it looks at the key, then the wizard, then the boss's spot, backing off to keep the party in view and reaching twice as far while the wizard stands, instead of cutting back to the level camera when the wizard leaves and leaving the party struggling to gather the coins.
- The good wizard and the boss's key are drawn in distance order, as the original sorts them, so the key behind Sumner no longer shows through him.
- Quitting a level from the pause menu goes straight back to the tower, without the level's tally and shop: the original kills the party and skips the shop when nobody stands.
- The Castle Armory's free-standing weapon rack no longer shows through the parapet walls in front of it. Its picture is an overlay the level authored to skip the depth compare, which the original paints over anything nearer; it is now depth-tested and lifted a tenth of a unit toward the eye, so it still lies over the rack's face but stays behind nearer walls.
