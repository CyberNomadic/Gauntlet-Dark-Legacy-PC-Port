# 2.0.0-alpha.3

## Added

- A developer console. The ` key slides a black DOS-style screen down from the top edge, as wide as the four status boxes, and back up; Escape sends it up too. While it is down it holds every control. Lines typed at its prompt, which carries the name of the character at the keyboard, run its commands; `help` lists them. Up and Down walk the lines entered before, Left and Right move the caret, and Page Up, Page Down or the wheel scroll the output.
- Console commands: `debug_info` shows or hides the level debug overlay; `list` names every map, what `get` knows, what your character carries, and a level's enemies, great ones, generators, pickups and objects, for the level in play or any map named after it; `goto` travels to a map or a point in it, or moves the party to a point here; `unlock_tower` opens every wing and portal of the tower; `get` hands over keys, potions, gold, health, any powerup, experience or a level; `notarget` hides you from the enemies; `god` turns harm away; `hidehud` draws the view alone; `freecam` lets the camera off the party to fly with the keys and the pointer; `suicide` fells everyone standing.
- `spawn` stands things before your character: so many of a swarm kind at a tier, a golem, a general or a gargoyle in a form, a generator breeding a kind, the temple's or the underworld's mixed brood, a pickup of the level, or a copy of a level's object. A map named last brings a kind's voices, a great one's costume or the object from that level, its archives loaded once and kept.
- Page Up and Page Down are recognised keys.
- The README links the port's Discord server and its PlayPorted listing.
