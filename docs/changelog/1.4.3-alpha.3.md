# 1.4.3-alpha.3

## Added

## Fixed

- A boss's coins are worth what the original gives them: BossSpewCoins writes 500, 1000 and 5000 over the bronze, silver and gold records, which the level tables keep at a tenth, so the Plague Fiend's four silver and one gold coins earn 9000 a player rather than 900.
- The Twisted Roots' runestone rides the platform that drops onto it. A placed item now rests on whichever floor, the level's starting pose or its authored pose, lies nearest its authored height, as FloorPos finds the floor an item was set on; the rune was being set in the pit seven units beneath the platform's rest height and then buried when the lever brought the platform down.
- An unseen body keeps writing depth while it is drawn faded, as the original's does, so the level's sorted scenery drawn after it (the Spider's floorboards and webs) no longer paints over it.
- A boss level's entrance camera hands over to the boss camera in the twenty ticks every entrance takes instead of gliding a quarter unit a tick, which from the Spider's marker a hundred units off took seconds before control returned.
- A lever, pad, chest or pickup rests on the floor nearest its authored height. The Branches' lever, authored on its bridge, was lifted onto a ledge's edge within a unit of it four units up and hung in the air beside the torch; the Fields' upper-landing call switch still keeps its landing over the lift deck eight units down.
- Your Worst Nightmare's swinging platform brings its lever and the golden claw up on its deck. Both were attached to the platform in its first-frame pose, ten units down and standing on end in its wall slot, so the swing carried them out five units beside the deck and left them on their sides out of reach; a marker or item now keeps the initialized world's floor unless the authored pose's lies a unit nearer its height, the floor it was set on.
- Under the Robotron keyboard scheme a held attack or throw no longer plants the body: it turns to the mouse and keeps walking the keys' way in the strafe gait a moving shot takes, sidestepping and backing as a pad's strafe does, so a shot can be aimed on the move. The slow swing keeps its creep as under any scheme.
