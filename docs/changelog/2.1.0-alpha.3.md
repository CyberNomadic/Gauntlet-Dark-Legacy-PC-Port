# 2.1.0-alpha.3

## Added

- `noclip` in the developer console flies your character through everything: the movement keys or the left stick along the ground as the camera looks, Q and E or the right stick for height, Shift to slow, with its speed eased the way the Source engine's is. A flier trips no pads, levers, pickups, chests or portals.
- The free camera turns with the right mouse button held, hiding the pointer where it is until the button is let go; Q and E take it up and down, Shift slows it, and its speed eases instead of stopping dead.
- With the console down, a click in the view selects what stands there, an object, enemy, generator, critter, pickup, player or spawned thing, marked out with an orange rim and a dull orange wash as gdl_toolkit marks its selection; it is kept as `$SELECTED` for later commands and let go when the console closes. `get info` describes the selection, or anything named by its token.

## Fixed

- The Temple's altar doors wait for their lever: the pads before them ride the lift it raises, authored five under the hall floor, and a body crossing the hall no longer reaches them. Trigger contact is measured as the original measures it, from the body's centre to a point a unit above the item's base.
- Model textures are drawn from their base image alone, as the GameCube draws them, so the mip levels made here no longer blur a clear surround into a sprite's edge: the border round the Temple's candles and the familiar's wings, and the Shipyard sheets' smear, go with them.
- `unlock_tower` opens the gargoyle statues' wings too, every kind's pieces kept as collected.
