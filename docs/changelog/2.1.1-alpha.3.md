# 2.1.1-alpha.3

## Added

## Fixed

- The Poison Fields' keys are all there: a key the level places with a count is a key ring worth that many, as the original places it, where every placed key was one. The ring by the note of traps for the greedy holds two, so the trapped chest by the locked gate no longer leaves a party without a key for it.
- The health an anti-death halo takes back from Death stops at the level's most, 9999 at the top, as every heal does; it went past it.
- The thirteenth runestone shows in the status box once held, with its own icon under the keys, as the original shows it.
- The warrior's pinball legend no longer throws its partner off a ledge: the thrown body stops at a floor's edge and at the edge of the shared view, as the original's does, where it sailed into the void and was stood again beside the warrior, or landed far off with the camera caught between the two.
- The dwarf's team attack works as the original's: the charger throws down the enemies it runs into (twenty a body, the pinball its own blow) and passes through them instead of being held up, and its flame stays alight in front of it for the whole ride.
- The coin room is dark again: a level flagged dark has its ambient light put out from the start, as the original has it, and the party's lanterns light it. A lantern's light now shows over a darkened level instead of being darkened with it.
- The Grounds' general no longer floats in mid-air: an enemy placed on a moving platform is seen and born where the platform is, riding it as the original's does, rather than where it was authored.
- A long inventory label is centred on the player's box and shrunk to fit between the frame's hooks, where it ran past the box.
