# 2.0.1-alpha.3

## Added

## Fixed

- The beast classes play on their own records: the minotaur, falconess, jackal, tigress, ogre, unicorn, medusa and hyena have fifty more than their base class on every base stat and a hundred more at the cap, as the original's PDATA gives them, where they were handed their base class's stats.
- The low swing is taken at the shrunk swarm: with the enemy shrinker on, every one of the swarm is a low melee target, as PlayerMotion judges a scaled-down enemy, where only those with a low hit height were. The great ones are unchanged.
- The Shipyard's half-seen sheets no longer hang over its ships: those pieces hold their texture coordinates at the clear edge of SKYDECK_CRACK on every vertex, so they show that edge alone, where the mip levels blended the planks beside it into a smear across the hull. A part pinned to a clamped edge of its texture is now sampled at the base level, as the PS2 draws it.
