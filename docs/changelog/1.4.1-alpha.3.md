# 1.4.1-alpha.3

## Added

## Fixed

- The runestone's landing sounds as the stone comes down in Sumner's ceremony, 91 ticks into its fall as the original cues it on the rune camera's count: it had been cued two seconds after the effect ended, during the pause for looking at the stone, where the next line covered it, so the fall seemed to end in silence.
- Looking into a chest with the X-Ray glasses shows its contents as they look lying about: the preview played none of the pickup's own animation and held its first frame, which for Growth is the potion at a tenth of its size and for a breath such as Lightning the moment before the icon fades in. The preview now plays the pickup's cycle and composites its glowing and facing parts smoothly, as pickups are drawn.
- An invisible or teleporting character is no longer hidden by generators and enemies standing behind it: a faded body writes no depth, so it is now drawn after them and blends over them.
