Amuse native MusyX engine
Source: https://github.com/derselbst/amuse/tree/503ad90e9857584f23d3389982f4883fa3190a5d

Native playback fixes from upstream commits:
https://github.com/derselbst/amuse/commit/9e34e4c87a1adbf7edd666bca5885d3c5ec99b9c
https://github.com/derselbst/amuse/commit/a7f7d64e8f83253e134ef3bdf0aecbdc557a4d2b
The standalone Nowide conversion call also passes its string through c_str().
Local native-playback changes implement the N64 REV_LEVEL sound-macro command
and reject unsupported commands or invalid macro counters instead of crashing.
The patch and its verified source hashes are in cmake/GdlPatchAmuseEffects.cmake.
